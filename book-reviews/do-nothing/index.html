"Do Nothing: How to Break Away from Overworking, Overdoing, and Underliving"

by Celeste Headlee

  • Read on 2026-09-05
  • Rating: ▲▲▲▲△
  • Format: Audio (7 hours 41 minutes)

I read this with a book club at work, on the heels of reading a book about acknowledging the time limits in (and of) your life - and encouraging you to consciously choose your boundaries and live life.

This book is similarly simple in its message: instead of filling your life being productive, you really should take some time to "do nothing" - do leisure activities, have a hobby, not "multi-task" while doing everything. You'll benefit from doing that in a lot of ways. I enjoyed the author's various methods of supporting that suggestion.

Although I run the club where this book was chosen, I don't actually vote on the books we read. Consequently, the combo of the books said more to me about some of the pressure the club members might feel in life. I can't deny my own guilt at some of the activities I've just described.

"Leviathan Falls"

by James S. A. Corey · The Expanse #9

  • Read on 2026-10-07
  • Rating: ▲▲▲▲▲

Since this is the conclusion of the series, let me review it first: 5 stars. Very enjoyable. This book was as good of a finale to the series as one could hope for, with some conclusions, and some loose ends. Yes, it's "sci-fi", but if that label is what stops you, then just know that you'll miss out on a good story involving interpersonal relationships, family dynamics, political complications, and... okay, some geeky stuff as well.

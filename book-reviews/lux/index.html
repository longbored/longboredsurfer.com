"Lux"

by Brandon Sanderson, Steven Michael Bohls · Texas Reckoners #1

  • Read on 2026-09-11
  • Rating: ▲▲▲▲△
  • Format: Audio (13 hours 54 minutes)

This is a great side-series to the main series of "The Reckoners" by Brandon Sanderson. I really liked the main series, and surely enjoyed this as well. It's the story of another set of insurgents, trying to fight against humans suddenly granted superhuman powers by the appearance of "Calamity" - a new red light/star in the sky.

There are a variety of enjoyable characters, and it's both something familiar combined with something entirely new. Good times.

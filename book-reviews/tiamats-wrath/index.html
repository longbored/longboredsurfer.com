A perfect setup for the next book - the series finale. I've appreciate the way the stories keep me engaged. At the samem time, I'm glad they're keeping things grounded in what might come from a human, or even "plausible". Yes, I know this is sci-fi, but I hate reading books with way too many coincidences or completely random elements coming into play out of nowhere. On to the next.

"The Nightingale"

by Kristin Hannah

  • Read on 2022-12-09
  • Rating: ▲▲▲▲▲

I loved seeing a civilian side of WWII - even if largely embellished. I'd like to think that I could be as brave as these people were if I were in similar circumstances, but that's no small step to make. The story of these sisters is 100% worth reading.

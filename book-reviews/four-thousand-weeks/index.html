"Four Thousand Weeks: Time Management for Mortals"

by Oliver Burkeman

  • Read on 2026-07-04
  • Rating: ▲▲▲▲△
  • Format: Audio (6 hours 13 minutes)

About 77 years, or four thousand weeks - that's how long you're likely to live. The author, Oliver Burkeman, contends that productivity systems, hacks, time management systems not only fail to address our problem with time management, but make things worse. Why worse? Because they all avoid the truth that we'll never be able to do everything - and no amount of becoming more efficient changes that. The way out is to accept the limited time we actually have, and make intentional choices about what to do and what not to do.

The appendix has "Ten Tools for Embracing Your Finitude," which help to embrace that mentality. I feel like those could be summarized into two broad tips:

  1. Choose boundary, and live it.
  2. Stop escaping, and meet the moment as it is.

I don't know what I thought this book would be, but it wasn't what it ended up being - and I was pleasantly surprised by that.

"Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies"

by Nick Bostrom

  • Read on 2026-10-30
  • Rating: ▲▲▲△△
  • Format: Audio (14 hours 17 minutes)

This book was really dense. I discussed this book every two weeks with a group of people at work, as our industry is being heavily influenced by AI. I needed to remind myself frequently that the book was written in 2016. The author made a number of guesses and estimations correctly, but there were a variety of elements that the author didn't address. The group conversations were interesting. Some people had a doom-and-gloom perspective almost the entire time. No matter how impressive AI is, and some of the potentials scenarios the author discusses, I couldn't help but think of some of those aspects that truly make something alive. So, while I am fascinated by AI, blown away by the speed at which things are moving, and intrigued by what the future holds, I'm comfortable we are nowhere near true "superintelligence" as this book discusses.

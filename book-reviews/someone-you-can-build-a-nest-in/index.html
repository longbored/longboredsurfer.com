"Someone You Can Build a Nest In"

by John Wiswell

  • Read on 2026-10-31
  • Rating: ▲▲▲△△
  • Format: Audio (9 hours 36 minutes)

This was certainly a fantasy novel, but it was also a conversation about relationships and social situations. The author created some interesting characters, predominantly among them is the main character of the book. I know that the main character is a monster, and yet throughout the book, I couldn't help but see their humanity. Some parts of the book were really weird. Other parts were super creative. If you're looking for a slightly different take on some fantasy, this might be worth considering.

Stephen Leeds has a special ability to essentially allow multiple personalities to take on essentially a complete persona - able to specialize in a certain area, and become an expert. That comes in handy for Stephen's private detective work. It's a book with more than just a clever title - an engaging story, some mystery, and a unique protagonist... or team of protagonists.
